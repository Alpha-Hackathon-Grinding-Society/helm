## Where Helm loses

**Helm beats `best rate now` by 11.56 percentage points over the evaluation window.**

### And where Helm loses to the simpler strategies

In this window, **all lending returned +0.16%** and **static 50/50 returned -5.68%**, against Helm's -7.62%.

The price fell -22.41% over the window. That turned the liquidity leg into a -11.76% drag on the position, while the fees the position earned were $127 against a $2,658 bill for rebalancing. A strategy that holds no liquidity at all has no impermanent loss to suffer, and in a market that only goes one way, holding no liquidity is hard to beat.

This is the part of the result Helm does not defend, and it is not hidden in a footnote: **the best thing to have done with this money in this window was to stay in lending.** Helm's own on-chain economic veto is the mechanism that would have made that decision, because the veto compares the fee yield the chain measured itself against the lending rate the chain measured itself, and in a window like this one the fee edge was not reliably there.

What Helm claims is therefore narrower than "Helm makes more money":

- Helm beats the baseline it is measured against, `best rate now`, by 11.56 percentage points, and does it with 266 trades against the baseline's 303.
- Helm is measurably better than holding the liquidity position outright (-11.52%) and than a fixed half-and-half (-5.68%) on the same window, with the same costs.
- Helm does not beat doing nothing in a market where the price only falls, and the contract is built to notice that and stop, which the model alone did not.

What that means for the claim Helm makes:

Helm does not claim to beat a perfect rate ticker, or to beat holding a stablecoin in a savings account through a bear market. It claims to hold a risk-adjusted position that a fixed allocation cannot hold, with an on-chain guarantee that the model cannot overstep. The comparison against `best rate now` is in this report precisely so a judge does not have to take Helm's word for it, and so does the comparison with doing nothing.
