## The backtest

Out of sample, on the held-out rows of real Base mainnet history for the Uniswap v3 ETH/USDC pool above. Reproduce with `agent/.venv/bin/python -m helm_agent.train`.

| Strategy | Total return | Max drawdown | Trades | Fees earned | Rebalance cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Helm (model + contract rules)** | -4.26% | -5.09% | 144 | $69.59 | $2,083.18 |
| Helm (model alone) | -4.91% | -6.06% | 126 | $91.68 | $1,077.16 |
| Best rate now | -19.18% | -20.14% | 303 | $182.67 | $13,825.23 |
| Static 50/50 | -5.68% | -8.06% | 0 | $129.02 | $0.00 |
| All lending | 0.16% | 0.00% | 0 | $0.00 | $0.00 |
| All LP | -11.52% | -16.01% | 0 | $258.03 | $0.00 |

**Helm beats "best rate now" by 14.91 points** of total return (-4.26% against -19.18%), with 144 trades against the baseline's 303.
**Helm loses to "all lending" by 4.43 points** (-4.26% against 0.16%). This is stated in the README and the dashboard, not hidden: the evaluation window is a falling market, and staying in lending is the safe thing to do in it.
**Helm beats "static 50/50" by 1.41 points** (-4.26% against -5.68%), and pays $2,083.18 of rebalance cost over 144 trades to do it.
The contract's own economic veto, applied on top of the model's proposals, is worth 0.64 points on this window: the model alone returned -4.91%, and the same model with the veto returned -4.26%. The rule was already deployed and already firing on chain; nothing was added to the model to produce it.

### The contract's economic veto on real market data

The veto fires when the fee yield the chain measured does not beat the lending rate by 50 bps. On the real pool used by this backtest it would have fired in **64% of the evaluation window**: the measured fee yield was below lending in 60% of the bars, and the median measured yield was 226 bps against a lending rate of 300 bps.

The live deployment measures a different pool — the mock LP pool on Base Sepolia, whose flow is far smaller — so the contract's own measured fee yield on the testnet is much smaller and its veto fires far more often. The rule is the same; the pool behind it is not.
